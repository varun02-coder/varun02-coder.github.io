Daily Wage Ledger

Clients

Each client has their own daily pay. A new rate applies to days you mark from now on. Days already marked keep their old rate.

Marking days for

Tap a day once for a full day, twice for a half day, three times to clear it. Tapping a day that belongs to another client moves it to the client chosen above.

Payments received

Paid before you worked the days? Add it the same way. That client shows as an advance until those days are marked.

    Backup

    Your records are saved only on this phone. If the phone is lost or changed, they are gone unless you have a backup. Send a backup to yourself on WhatsApp every week. On a new phone, tap Restore and choose that file.
